How this works

It's just me and a wood-fired oven out back. I make the dough from scratch — a sourdough that cold-ferments for three days before it hits the fire, which is what gives the crust its blister, chew, and a little tang.

Since it's a one-person thing, I make one kind of pizza each week in a limited batch. I'll post the date, what I'm making, and the price for that night — when it's up, you'll see it right here.

Reserve a spot with just your name, pick a pickup time, and tell me how many you want. Come by hungry and text me when you pull up — I'll bring them out hot.
